# Mission 1 3D assets

The following models are used locally by the Mission 1 workshop scene. The computer models are from the “Computers and Desk Gadgets” pack by 3DAssets.dev. The workbench is a separate Innerscene PBR asset, and the HDRI lighting is from Poly Haven. Each source identifies these assets as CC0/public domain. Local copies keep gameplay independent of the source CDNs.

Pack: https://3dassets.dev/packs/computers-and-desk-gadgets
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
